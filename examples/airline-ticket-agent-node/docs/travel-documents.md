# Travel Documents and Entry Requirements

## Passports
Most destinations require a passport valid for at least six months beyond your
date of entry. Some countries also require two blank facing pages. It is the
passenger's responsibility to hold correct documentation; passengers refused
entry are not entitled to a refund of the fare.

## Visas
Visa requirements depend on your nationality and destination, not on the
airline you fly. Many countries offer visa-free entry or visa on arrival for
short stays, while others require a visa arranged in advance. Electronic travel
authorisations such as ESTA for the United States and ETA for Canada must be
approved before departure and are checked at the gate.

Passengers transiting through a third country may need a transit visa even
without leaving the airport. Check the rules for every country on your
itinerary, not only your final destination.

## Travelling with children
Children of any age need their own passport. Some countries require a notarised
letter of consent when a child travels with only one parent or with someone who
is not a parent. Airlines are required to deny boarding where documentation is
incomplete.

## Health requirements
A small number of destinations require proof of yellow fever vaccination,
particularly for passengers arriving from countries where the disease is
present. Check requirements at least six weeks before departure, as some
vaccinations must be given well in advance to be considered valid.
