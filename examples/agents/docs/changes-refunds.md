# Changes, Cancellations and Refunds

## The 24-hour window
Any ticket booked at least seven days before departure may be cancelled within
24 hours of purchase for a full refund, regardless of fare type. This applies
to Basic fares as well and is the simplest way to correct a booking mistake.

## Changing your flight
Flex and Business tickets can be changed free of charge; you pay only the
difference in fare if the new flight costs more. Standard tickets carry a 50
USD change fee plus the fare difference. Basic tickets cannot be changed at
all, and the value of the ticket is lost if you do not travel.

Changes can be made online through Manage My Booking up to three hours before
departure. After that, changes must be made by phone or at the airport.

## Cancellations
Flex and Business tickets are fully refundable to the original payment method,
typically within seven business days. Standard tickets are refundable as travel
credit valid for twelve months, less a 75 USD cancellation fee. Basic tickets
are not refundable, though government taxes may still be reclaimed.

## When we cancel
If we cancel a flight or delay it by more than five hours, you may choose a
full cash refund or rebooking on the next available service at no cost.
Depending on the route and distance, you may also be entitled to statutory
compensation under passenger rights regulations, separate from the refund.

## Name corrections
Minor spelling corrections of up to three characters are free. A genuine change
of passenger name is not permitted; the ticket must be cancelled and rebooked
under the fare rules above.
