# Frequent Flyer Programme

## Earning miles
Members earn miles on every flight based on distance flown and fare type. Basic
fares earn 25 percent of distance, Standard 100 percent, Flex 150 percent and
Business 250 percent. Miles are credited within seven days of travel and can be
claimed retroactively for up to six months if your number was missing from the
booking.

## Tier levels
Silver requires 25,000 tier miles in a calendar year and grants an extra
checked bag plus priority check-in. Gold requires 50,000 tier miles and adds
lounge access, priority boarding and free seat selection on all fare types.
Platinum requires 100,000 tier miles and adds guaranteed availability on sold
out flights booked at least 48 hours ahead, plus a companion upgrade each year.

Tier status is valid for the year in which it is earned plus the following full
calendar year.

## Spending miles
Award tickets start at 12,000 miles one way on short-haul routes and 45,000
miles on long-haul economy. Business class awards start at 90,000 miles one
way. Award availability is limited on peak dates and we recommend booking as
early as possible, since award seats are released eleven months in advance.

Miles can also be used for seat upgrades, extra baggage and lounge passes.
Upgrades using miles are only permitted from Standard fares or higher; Basic
fares cannot be upgraded.

## Expiry
Miles expire after 24 months of account inactivity. Any earning or spending
activity resets the clock on your entire balance.
