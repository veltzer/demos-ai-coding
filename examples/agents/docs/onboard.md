# Onboard Experience

## Food and drink
Long-haul flights include a hot meal, a second lighter service before landing,
and complimentary soft drinks, beer and wine throughout. Short-haul economy
offers a buy-on-board menu of sandwiches, snacks and hot drinks; card payment
only, no cash accepted.

Special meals -- vegetarian, vegan, kosher, halal, gluten-free, low-sodium and
child meals -- can be requested free of charge up to 24 hours before departure
through Manage My Booking. Requests made after that cannot be guaranteed.

## Entertainment and connectivity
Long-haul aircraft have seat-back screens with films, television and music.
Short-haul aircraft stream to your own device over onboard wifi; download the
app before you fly, as the app store is not reachable in the air.

Wifi is free for messaging on all aircraft. Full internet access costs 12 USD
per flight for economy passengers and is included for Business class and Gold
and Platinum members. Power outlets and USB ports are available at every seat
on long-haul aircraft and in the front six rows on short-haul.

## Comfort
Cabin temperature is kept between 22 and 24 degrees Celsius. Blankets and
pillows are provided on all long-haul flights and available on request on
short-haul night flights. Cabin air is fully exchanged every two to three
minutes and passes through HEPA filters that remove more than 99.9 percent of
airborne particles.

## Onboard rules
Smoking and vaping are prohibited on all flights, including in lavatories.
Alcohol brought aboard by passengers may not be consumed. Portable electronic
devices may be used throughout the flight in flight mode; larger devices must
be stowed for takeoff and landing. Spare lithium batteries and power banks must
travel in the cabin and never in checked baggage.
