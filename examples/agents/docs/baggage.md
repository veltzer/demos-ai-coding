# Baggage Policy

## Carry-on
Every passenger may bring one cabin bag no larger than 56 x 36 x 23 cm,
including wheels and handles, plus one small personal item such as a handbag or
laptop case that fits under the seat in front. Cabin bags are limited to 8 kg on
economy fares and 12 kg in business class. Basic economy tickets include the
personal item only; a full cabin bag costs 45 USD when added online and 75 USD
if purchased at the gate.

## Checked baggage
Economy fares include one checked bag up to 23 kg. Business class includes two
bags up to 32 kg each. Additional bags cost 60 USD each when added before
departure. Bags between 23 kg and 32 kg incur a 100 USD overweight charge, and
we cannot accept any single bag heavier than 32 kg.

## Special items
Skis and snowboards are not accepted on any of our flights, in the cabin or as
checked baggage, and cannot be carried for any fee. Passengers travelling with
ski equipment must arrange separate freight shipping. Other sports equipment
such as golf clubs and bicycles travels as a checked bag but must be declared
at least 24 hours before departure. Musical instruments too large for the
overhead bin require a purchased extra seat. Fragile items, cash, jewellery,
electronics and medication should always travel in your cabin bag; we cannot
accept liability for these in checked baggage.

## Delayed or damaged bags
Report missing or damaged baggage at the arrivals desk before leaving the
airport. Most delayed bags are located within 24 hours and delivered to your
address at no charge. Claims for damage must be filed within seven days of
arrival.
