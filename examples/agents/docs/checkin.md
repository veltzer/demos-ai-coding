# Check-in and Boarding

## When to check in
Online check-in opens 48 hours before departure and closes 90 minutes before
departure for international flights, 60 minutes for domestic. Airport desks
open three hours before international departures and close 60 minutes before
takeoff. We strongly recommend checking in online and arriving at the airport
three hours early for international travel, two hours for domestic.

## Boarding passes
Mobile boarding passes are accepted at every airport we serve except Lagos and
Kathmandu, where a printed pass is required. Save your pass to your phone
wallet before leaving for the airport in case of poor connectivity in the
terminal.

## Boarding sequence
Boarding begins 40 minutes before departure and closes 15 minutes before
departure. Passengers requiring assistance and families with young children
board first, followed by business class and Flex fares, then rear rows to front
rows in groups. The gate closes 15 minutes before scheduled departure and late
passengers cannot be accommodated, even if they have already checked in.

## Missed connections
If you miss a connection because an inbound flight of ours arrived late, we
will rebook you on the next available service at no charge and provide meal
vouchers for waits over three hours, plus hotel accommodation for overnight
delays. Connections booked as separate tickets are not protected.
