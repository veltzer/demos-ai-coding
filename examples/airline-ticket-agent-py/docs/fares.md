# Fare Types and Pricing

## Fare families
We sell four fare types on most routes.

**Basic** is the lowest price. Seats are assigned automatically at check-in, no
cabin bag is included, and the ticket cannot be changed or refunded.

**Standard** includes a cabin bag, one checked bag and free seat selection from
48 hours before departure. Changes are permitted for a 50 USD fee plus any fare
difference.

**Flex** includes everything in Standard plus free changes, free seat selection
at any time, priority boarding and a full refund if cancelled before departure.

**Business** includes lie-flat seating on long-haul aircraft, lounge access,
two checked bags, priority everything and fully flexible conditions.

## How prices move
Fares generally rise as the departure date approaches. The cheapest fares are
typically found between four and eight weeks before travel. Tuesday and
Wednesday departures usually cost less than Friday and Sunday departures, and
early morning or late evening flights are cheaper than midday ones.

Peak periods -- school holidays, national holidays and the summer months of
July and August -- carry significantly higher fares, often double the low
season price on the same route.

## Taxes and surcharges
Displayed prices include all mandatory taxes and airport charges. Payment by
credit card carries no additional fee. Optional extras such as seat selection,
extra baggage and travel insurance are added at the final stage of booking.
