# Seating and Cabin Comfort

## Choosing a seat
Seat selection is free at any time on Flex and Business fares, free from 48
hours before departure on Standard fares, and chargeable on Basic fares. Prices
range from 12 USD for a standard economy seat to 60 USD for extra-legroom rows
on long-haul services.

## Seat types
Standard economy seats offer 79 cm of pitch on most aircraft. Extra-legroom
seats at exit rows and bulkheads offer 91 cm. Exit row seats carry safety
requirements: passengers must be over 15, physically able to operate the door,
and cannot be seated there if travelling with an infant or requiring an
extension seatbelt.

Business class on long-haul aircraft has lie-flat seats in a 1-2-1
configuration, giving every passenger direct aisle access. On short-haul
aircraft, business class uses standard economy seating with the adjacent middle
seat blocked.

## Travelling together
Passengers on the same booking are seated together automatically where possible
without charge, and children under 12 are always seated with an accompanying
adult at no cost. If you book separately and want to sit together, add seat
selection to each ticket.

## Bassinets and accessibility
Bassinet positions at bulkhead rows can be requested free for infants under 11
kg but cannot be guaranteed until check-in. Passengers requiring wheelchair
assistance, an extension seatbelt or an onboard oxygen supply should notify us
at least 48 hours before departure so we can prepare the appropriate equipment.
